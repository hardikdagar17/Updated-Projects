Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions our customers ask most often

How long does delivery take across India? ▼
We dispatch all orders within 24 hours. Metro cities typically receive deliveries within 2 to 3 business days, while non-metro locations take 4 to 5 business days. You will receive an SMS and email with live courier tracking upon dispatch.
What is your 7-Day Replacement Policy? ▼
If your item arrives damaged, defective, or different from described, you can initiate a return or replacement within 7 days of delivery. Our courier partner will pick up the package from your doorstep without extra charges.
How do I apply coupon codes like HEXA10? ▼
You can open your slide-out cart by clicking the cart bag in the header. Enter "HEXA10" into the Promo Code box and click Apply. Your 10% discount will immediately reflect on the subtotal.
Are all HEXA products covered under warranty? ▼
Yes! All HEXA smartwatches, audio devices, chargers, and kitchen appliances come with a minimum 1-Year Comprehensive Manufacturer Warranty against manufacturing defects.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept all major UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), Net Banking across 50+ Indian banks, and Cash on Delivery (COD) on select pin codes.
